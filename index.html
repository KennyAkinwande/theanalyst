About

Kenny is a multidisciplinary healthcare professional with expertise in health technology, clinical practice, healthcare operations and health education, bridging healthcare practice, technology and organizational strategy.

I work where health technology, business analysis, clinical practice, healthcare operations, education and research meet. What drives me is solving complex problems: improving how organisations work, and translating business needs into practical, technology-enabled solutions.

I began my project management journey in 2022 and have since expanded into blended project delivery and business analysis, working on both the strategic and the operational side of a problem. My experience spans public-sector clinical skills development, healthcare education, research collaborations and consulting engagements inside and outside Canada. I have also taught and mentored more than 300 healthcare professionals and leaders.

My approach: define the problem, engage the right stakeholders, understand the current process and analyse the data. Then identify the gaps, document business and functional requirements, and recommend solutions that improve efficiency, strengthen decision-making and enhance service delivery.

Meaningful transformation happens when clinical expertise, business strategy, technology and data work together. My goal is to bridge these disciplines, helping organisations move from complex challenges to better processes and measurable outcomes.
